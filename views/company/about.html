About the Company

Artificial intelligence is moving beyond the cloud.
We are building the infrastructure that takes it there.

Atomo Innovation Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian deep-tech company developing integrated edge-AI computing platforms for industries, infrastructure, enterprises, and intelligent environments.

Our platform combines purpose-built computing hardware, AtomicOS, local AI inference, multi-protocol connectivity, and application software into one unified ecosystem.

By processing intelligence close to where data is created, Atomo helps organisations respond faster, protect sensitive information, reduce cloud dependence, and deploy AI in environments where reliability matters.

We believe the next phase of computing will not exist only inside data centres. It will operate inside factories, cities, buildings, vehicles, healthcare systems, farms, and machines.

Our Vision

To build one of the world’s most important computing ecosystems for Physical AI—originating in India and serving the world.

We envision a future where intelligent machines and infrastructure can understand, decide, and act locally. Our ambition is to make secure, efficient, and accessible edge intelligence a foundational layer of the global computing industry.

Our Mission

To make powerful AI computing available wherever the physical world creates data.

We integrate edge-AI hardware, operating systems, connectivity, developer tools, and real-world applications into a unified platform that enables organisations to deploy intelligence securely, efficiently, and at scale.

The Atomo Ecosystem

Hardware. Software. Intelligence. One integrated platform.

From industrial-grade silicon to developer tools and cloud orchestration — the Atomo ecosystem connects every layer of edge intelligence into one unified platform built for the physical world.

Why Now

The physical world generates intelligence faster than the cloud can respond.

Atomo brings intelligence closer to action.

  1. Latency

    Real-world systems often need decisions in milliseconds, not after a cloud round trip.

  2. Privacy

    Sensitive video, industrial, healthcare, and operational data should not always leave the location where it is created.

  3. Reliability

    Factories, infrastructure, machines, and safety systems must continue operating even when internet connectivity is weak or unavailable.

  4. Efficiency

    Sending every frame, sensor event, or machine signal to the cloud creates unnecessary bandwidth, cost, and energy consumption.

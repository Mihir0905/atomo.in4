Edge Safety Monitoring on Active Construction Sites

Infrastructure construction firm

Overview

On-site vision and sensor fusion for PPE compliance and hazard awareness — intelligence where the site operates.

Challenge

Large, dynamic sites with intermittent connectivity needed safety intelligence without relying on fragile cloud links or delayed review of footage.

Solution

Rugged edge hardware runs safety models locally. Supervisors receive real-time alerts; incident clips are stored and synced when links are available.

Outcomes

  • Earlier visibility into safety events on site
  • Operations continue when uplink is limited
  • Centralized fleet management via Atomic Center

Local Traffic & Crowd Intelligence for Urban Systems

Municipal smart-infrastructure program

Overview

Camera analytics at intersections and public spaces — processed locally for real-time decisions and reduced bandwidth load.

Challenge

City operators required real-time event detection across distributed camera networks. Continuous cloud upload of HD streams was impractical and raised data-sovereignty concerns.

Solution

Distributed edge nodes with AtomicOS run detection models on-site. Atomic Center provides fleet visibility, OTA updates, and policy-controlled sync.

Outcomes

  • Lower bandwidth use across camera networks
  • Local alerting for operators and field teams
  • Scalable deployment across multiple sites

Real-Time Quality Inspection on the Factory Floor

Industrial manufacturer (India)

Overview

Edge AI vision deployed on the production line for defect detection without sending every camera frame to the cloud.

Challenge

Quality teams needed millisecond-level inspection on high-throughput lines. Cloud round-trips introduced latency, bandwidth costs, and downtime risk when connectivity dropped.

Solution

Atomo Processing Units with AtomicOS and ASNN SDK run machine-vision models locally. Camera streams are processed at the edge; only alerts and summaries sync upstream.

Outcomes

  • Faster anomaly response on active production lines
  • Reduced dependency on continuous cloud connectivity
  • Privacy-preserving processing inside the facility

Platform

ElectronAtomicOSASNN SDK
